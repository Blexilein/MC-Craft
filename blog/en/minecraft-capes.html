About Minecraft Capes

Facts about the exclusive capes

What are capes?

Capes are special cloaks only awarded by Mojang. They are purely cosmetic items with no gameplay effects.

Obtaining Capes

Capes are awarded for special achievements: MineCon attendance, translation help, working at Mojang or special events.

Important Note

Official capes cannot be purchased or obtained through mods. Unauthorized cape mods lead to account bans.

Cross-Platform

Capes are visible on all platforms (Java & Bedrock) and are synchronized via your Microsoft account.
