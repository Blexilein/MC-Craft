The Recipe Book

Every recipe in Minecraft Java Edition

Items

Good to know

  • Cycling ingredients stand for a group, such as any wooden planks. Hover over one to pause it.
  • Click an ingredient to jump straight to its recipes. “As ingredient” lists every item that recipes need.
  • /recipe give @s * unlocks every recipe in the recipe book at once.
  • Every item has its own link – share it with the link button next to the name.

How crafting works

Five workstations, one recipe book

Shaped or shapeless

Shaped recipes need the right layout in the grid, shapeless ones only the ingredients. Recipes up to 2×2 also work in the inventory.

Four ways to cook

The blast furnace smelts ores twice as fast, the smoker does the same for food. The campfire needs no fuel but takes 30 seconds.

Brewing & smithing

Potions start as water bottles: redstone extends them, glowstone makes them stronger. The smithing table turns diamond into netherite or trims armour.
