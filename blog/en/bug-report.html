Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about bug reports

How long does a response on Discord take?

Usually within 15-60 minutes during our support hours (Mon-Fri 10:00-18:00). On weekends it may take a little longer.

What happens after I report a bug?

1. Our team checks your report
2. Bug is categorized and prioritized
3. Development to fix begins
4. You receive updates in the Discord channel

Can I report multiple bugs at once?

It's best to report each bug in a separate message. This helps us track and fix them faster.

What information should I have ready?

• Which tool is affected
• Detailed error description
• Screenshots or videos
• Browser and operating system
• Steps to reproduce

Got more questions? Visit our FAQ page or contact us directly.
