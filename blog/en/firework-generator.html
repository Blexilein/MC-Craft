Commands

Import a command

Paste a /give or /summon command – the new format (1.20.5+) and the old NBT format are both recognised.

Good to know

  • With one gunpowder up to seven stars fit into the crafting table, with three gunpowder still five. A recipe always gives three rockets.
  • Rockets with stars deal damage when they explode: 5 plus 2 per star, weaker up to 5 blocks away. For flying with elytra, use rockets without stars.
  • Three or more stars or a large ball make a louder bang. Twinkle makes the firework last 15 ticks longer and ends with a crackle.
  • A dispenser fires rockets in the direction it faces – point it up and wire it with redstone to build whole firework shows.
  • Drag to turn the camera and use the mouse wheel to zoom. A click into the preview launches a rocket.

How your firework is made

Three steps at the crafting table

Craft a star

Gunpowder, at least one dye and optionally a shape ingredient, a diamond for the trail and glowstone dust for the twinkle.

Add a fade

Put the finished star into the crafting table with more dyes – the sparks then change to these colours as they burn out.

Build the rocket

Paper, one to three gunpowder for the flight duration and your stars make three rockets.
