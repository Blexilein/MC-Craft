Staircased uses height steps (one step up/down/same) for extra light/dark shading – this unlocks far more usable colors than a flat single layer, though the build can end up at varying heights.

Block colors are computed from this site's own block render images, not from Mojang's internal map color table – in individual cases the color may therefore differ slightly.

Downloads go through the Structure Converter – .litematic loads directly into the Litematica mod.

Official Minecraft Map Colors

The game's official base color set (62 colors, 4 brightness levels each – level 2 is the full/normal color). map.dat mode matches directly against this table.

All Blocks & Their Color

Our block palette is finer-grained than the 62 official map colors, since every block gets its own color computed from its render image.
