Current Version

All final changes for the official 1.2.0 release

v1.2.0 September 17, 2026

Release 1.2.0: New Tools, New Interface & Public API

New Features

  • NEWNew Firework Generator: build rockets with all five shapes, colours, fade colours, trail and twinkle – with a preview that calculates the particles like Minecraft (including sounds and subtitles), crafting steps that point out what only works with commands, presets, a share link, importing existing commands and /give and /summon commands in the new and old format
  • NEWNew Armor Trim Generator: try all 18 trim patterns and 11 materials on every armor – with a 3D preview on the player (Steve or Alex), a custom colour for leather armor, inventory icons, /give and armor stand commands and every place to find the smithing templates with its chance
  • NEWNew Schematic Viewer: view .schem, .schematic, .litematic and .nbt files in 3D – with real block models and textures from Minecraft 26.3, a layer slider, block info on hover, a material list (copy or CSV), screenshots and four example builds; the file stays on your device
  • NEWNew Crafting Recipes page: all 2,042 recipes from Minecraft 26.3 for the crafting table, furnace, blast furnace, smoker, campfire, stonecutter, smithing table and brewing stand – with search, an “As ingredient” view, cycling ingredients for groups, potion effects, in-game icons and a copyable /recipe command
  • NEWNew Sign Generator: write on signs and hanging signs of every wood type with colours, a custom colour, formatting, glowing text and click commands – with an in-game preview, PNG export and ready-made /setblock, /give and /data commands for Minecraft 1.20 to 26.3, plus a command block guide
  • NEWNew Head Texture Generator: textures of items, blocks, mobs, villagers and player skins as a player head – with a 3D preview, a ready-made /give command, its own scrolling list and a button to stop the rotation
  • NEWNew Nether Coordinate Calculator: converts Overworld and Nether coordinates both ways (÷ 8 / × 8), with copy buttons
  • NEWNew Minecraft Command Generator: click together /give, /summon, /effect, enchanted items with several enchantments, /teleport, /gamemode, /weather and /time – items and mobs are picked by category from the databases
  • NEWMobs Database: new “3D Model” tab – rotate every mob in 3D, with all texture variants (babies too), removable saddle and bridle and a button to stop the rotation
  • NEWMobs Database: new “Sounds” tab with every official sound and subtitle – play each variant on its own, hold to loop
  • NEWMobs Database: new “Game Data”, “Drops”, “Spawning” and “Variants” tabs – stats, size comparison, equipment, loot with chances, biomes and structures, food and taming straight from the game
  • NEWItems Database: textures (animated ones too) and sounds for every item and block
  • NEWItems Database: info box for music discs with title, artist, length, first Minecraft version with its date, comparator signal and a link to a YouTube search
  • NEWItems Database: sounds show the elapsed time and the total length while playing, e.g. 0:12 / 2:58
  • NEWCapes Database: new “Dungeons II” category with all five Minecraft Dungeons II capes – Hero Cape and Twisted Cape (also for Java & Bedrock) with Mojang’s original textures and the 3D view, plus Soul Cape, Corrupted Creeper Cape and Special Cape, which only exist in Dungeons II – shown with their official image and a notice in the viewer, since they have no Minecraft texture
  • NEWThe “Coming Soon” page is split up: finished tools with a check mark and a link, planned tools on their own – the progress bar works itself out
  • NEWA new interface on every page: header, navigation, hero section, buttons and footer have been redesigned and now look the same across all 90 pages – in all three themes
  • NEWNew hero section: a blueprint grid in the background and a glow in the theme colors that changes with Nether and End; the title scales smoothly to any screen size, and on phones the four tiles sit in a compact 2×2 grid
  • NEWRedesigned tool cards on the homepage: icon plates in accent colors, every “Open Tool” link at the same height, and the whole card is clickable
  • NEWA tidier homepage: tools are now sorted into categories – Databases, Generators, Skin Editors, Converters & Viewers, Calculators & Helpers and Servers & Players – with a jump bar on top and an accent colour per category; the Minecraft section (capes, skins & End Poem) stays as it was
  • NEWFavicons in the style of each theme: the tab icon changes with the color theme (Overworld, Nether, End), and on phones the browser bar picks up the matching color too
  • NEW3D Skin Poser: load a skin by upload or username, drop it into ready-made poses or looping animations, and freely position, rotate, and scale individual limbs with sliders
  • NEWSkin Poser export as PNG, a 4-angle image sheet, or an animated GIF, including cape upload, a choice of background, and a personal "My Renders" gallery
  • NEWSkin Poser: new “Parts” tab – show or hide the head, body, arms and legs and each of their overlay layers individually, including “Show all parts” (like the Chibi Skin Maker)
  • NEWChibi Skin Maker: automatically turns any skin into a chibi pixel-art figure across 9 poses and 4 camera angles, exportable as PNG, GIF, APNG, or WebM – entirely in the browser
  • NEWMinecraft Heads database: a searchable collection of 70+ MHF heads plus logo heads, with a ready-to-copy /give command in 3 selectable syntax variants
  • NEWMap Art Generator: the materials list can now also be exported as a CSV or TXT file
  • NEWA first-party MC-Craft API now proxies Server Status, Skin Lookup, Skin Editor, Skin Poser, Minecraft Versions, and Minecraft API Status – replacing several direct third-party calls made from the browser
  • NEWNew page /blog/en/api-documentation.html: a full reference for the MC-Craft API covering every endpoint (Items, Mobs, Capes, Heads, Skins, Player, Server status, QR code and Advancement generator), parameter tables, and ready-made test links
  • NEWAPI documentation in a new developer design: panels in the style of the generator pages, GET badges, endpoint cards, code in a monospace font and clear parameter tables – in all three themes
  • NEWCopy buttons on every example URL in the API documentation – one click copies the finished link straight to the clipboard
  • NEWFooter: new “API Docs” button with a book icon in the Version · Changelog · API Docs · Cookies bar
  • NEWWelcome messages now also on the QR Generator, Minecraft Versions, Coming Soon and Advancement Generator pages
  • NEWStructured data (JSON-LD) for search engines on every page; the FAQ is marked up as an FAQ page

Improvements

  • IMPROVEDAll data updated to Minecraft 26.3 – new blocks such as poplar wood are now in the Head Texture Generator too
  • IMPROVEDEnglish Mobs Database complete: every text translated from the German version and the same textures – English now has as many mob heads as German
  • IMPROVEDItems and Mobs Database in one language throughout: German pages use the official German names, English pages only English
  • IMPROVEDThe Head Texture Generator recognises more official textures (e.g. light at all 16 levels, crossbow, compass, clock) and shows the adult texture of a mob first
  • IMPROVEDItem IDs match the game: red_shrub and piglin_banner_pattern
  • IMPROVEDNew tools added everywhere: navigation, homepage, FAQ, terms of use, privacy policy, copyright and sitemap
  • IMPROVEDA shared design and script foundation for all 90 pages: header, navigation, footer, themes, notifications and core logic now live in one central place – about 23,000 lines of CSS and 12,000 lines of JavaScript less duplicated code
  • IMPROVEDCalmer spacing and better typography: hero descriptions are no longer all caps, long page titles are hyphenated cleanly instead of being cut off, and cards on the homepage stand out from the background again
  • IMPROVEDItems, Mobs, Capes, Skins, Advancement Generator, API Status, Minecraft Versions, QR Generator, Server Status, Skin Editor, and Skin Lookup now run on one shared German/English module instead of separate files
  • IMPROVEDA single, noticeably faster loading screen across all pages instead of many different progress bars
  • IMPROVEDToast notifications: calmer design, centered text and a smooth slide-in and fade-out
  • IMPROVEDMouse cursor: the diamond sword now only appears on plain areas; links and buttons show the hand, inputs and text the text cursor, the scrollbar the normal pointer – drag and resize cursors in the editors work again
  • IMPROVEDCode examples and URLs are shown in a monospace font site-wide
  • IMPROVEDSkin Poser: the export card looks the same in German and English, and the settings cards are no longer cut off on the right on phones
  • IMPROVEDAccessibility: a “Skip to main content” link, keyboard-friendly menus, and dropdowns that report their state to screen readers
  • IMPROVEDNavigation, mobile menu, and footer on every page now link to the API documentation
  • IMPROVEDMinecraft API Status now links directly to the full API documentation
  • IMPROVEDGerman and English blog pages now have native-language URLs – old links redirect automatically
  • IMPROVEDThe footer logo is now clickable on every page and leads back to the homepage
  • IMPROVEDMobile navigation no longer scrolls through into the page behind it
  • IMPROVEDUpdates arrive immediately: every file is now versioned, so the browser no longer keeps outdated copies after an update
  • IMPROVEDTheme-coloured scrollbar now in Firefox too
  • IMPROVEDNew security headers (including Content Security Policy and HSTS) plus a security.txt for responsible vulnerability disclosure
  • IMPROVEDPrivacy Policy updated to reflect the new first-party API architecture

Bug Fixes

  • FIXEDIn-page jump links (e.g. “All Tools”) now land right below the header, even when the target sits inside a nested section
  • FIXEDSkin Poser: the GIF now shows the chosen animation at the right speed
  • FIXEDChibi Skin Maker: the GIF had a green background and lost colours – transparency and colours are right now
  • FIXEDHead Texture Generator: see-through parts of the head showed as black
  • FIXEDItems Database: “Play all” and “Stop all” in the sounds did not work
  • FIXEDMobs Database: after switching to another mob, the 3D model only loaded after another click
  • FIXEDResizing the window threw an error on every event (“resizeTimeout is not defined”), which made some pages stutter
  • FIXEDSeveral pages showed raw translation keys such as “toast_skin_loaded” instead of text
  • FIXEDDuplicate notifications: the welcome message appeared twice, and the skin and cape galleries announced a selection as soon as the page loaded
  • FIXEDThe theme switcher and sound button did nothing in the Skin Editor and Beacon Color Mixer, the sound button on six more pages as well; the click sound played twice
  • FIXEDChibi Skin Maker: “Reset” returns to the starting view (¾ right) instead of the front, “Reset everything” keeps the style and outline, and the highlighted view matches the image
  • FIXEDHarmless browser notices no longer trigger a red error message
  • FIXEDMinecraft Heads (EN) threw an error on load; the ZIP export in the Chibi Skin Maker works again
  • FIXEDMinecraft Versions showed no notifications at all, and Server Status made a broken request on load
  • FIXEDBug report: the Discord notice showed “info” instead of its text
  • FIXEDToast notifications site-wide are now hardened against HTML injection
  • FIXEDThe English cookie banner incorrectly linked to a German-named privacy page instead of /blog/en/privacy-policy.html
  • FIXEDGradient headings could render invisible in some browsers – a fallback color was added
  • FIXEDDay/Night Cycle: dragging the time slider kept firing a click sound continuously
  • FIXEDHover effects were broken site-wide – tool cards, FAQ entries and stats react to the mouse again
  • FIXEDThe mobile menu could not be closed by clicking beside it – a dimmed backdrop now closes it with one click
  • FIXEDThe theme picker snapped shut immediately when opened with the keyboard

Notes

Version 1.2.0 brought eleven new tools (Skin Poser, Chibi Skin Maker, Minecraft Heads, Head Texture Generator, Nether Coordinate Calculator, Minecraft Command Generator, Sign Generator, Crafting Recipes, Schematic Viewer, Armor Trim Generator and Firework Generator) and a first-party, publicly documented API. The revision of September 10, 2026 added a new interface for every page, a shared technical foundation, many bug fixes and a round of polish. Up to September 17, the Mobs Database gained 3D models, sounds and game data, the Items Database textures, sounds and music disc details, all data moved to Minecraft 26.3, the homepage was sorted into categories and the Capes Database gained the Dungeons II capes.

v1.1.0 – Map Art Generator Expansion & Loading Animation August 29, 2026

  • NEWStructure Converter – converts structures between .schematic, .schem, .litematic, and .nbt directly in the browser
  • NEWmap.dat export in the Map Art Generator – produces a real Minecraft map file instead of a structure
  • NEWFree map size (up to 50×50 maps) and ZIP export with individual maps for large builds
  • NEWContrast control and dithering for better image quality on logos, text, and photos
  • NEWSearchable tables of every block color and the 62 official Minecraft map colors
  • NEWNew loading animation on page load, with a progress readout and the current version
  • IMPROVEDMore accurate color matching in the Map Art Generator via perceptually weighted color distance
  • IMPROVEDPreview now shows the original and result side by side for comparison
  • IMPROVEDBlock selection grouped by material category, filterable and toggleable per category
  • IMPROVEDSkin Editor promoted from Alpha to Beta
  • FIXEDTarget format in the Map Art Generator incorrectly reset to .litematic on every re-generation
  • FIXEDPreview image in the Map Art Generator sometimes rendered too large or stretched
  • FIXEDRed tones were sometimes incorrectly matched to orange blocks in color search

Version 1.1.0 significantly expanded the Map Art Generator (map.dat, ZIP export, image quality) and introduced a new loading animation.

  • NEWA unified modern design system across all pages
  • NEWFully responsive layout for smartphones, tablets, and desktops
  • NEWImproved navigation with consistent paths and controls

  • IMPROVEDBetter spacing, typography, and contrast for readability
  • IMPROVEDImproved touch targets and focus states
