Good to Know

  • 1 Nether block equals 8 Overworld blocks – that makes Nether portals great for covering long distances in the Overworld.
  • The Y coordinate (height) stays unchanged during conversion.
  • Overworld → Nether is rounded, since block coordinates are always whole numbers.
  • Portals that are less than 128 blocks apart in Nether coordinates often link to an existing portal instead of creating a new one.

How It Really Works

The math behind the Nether conversion

Overworld → Nether

Divide X and Z by 8 to get the matching Nether coordinate.

Nether → Overworld

Multiply X and Z by 8 to get the Overworld coordinate.

Y Stays the Same

Height (Y) is not scaled – it is identical in both dimensions.
