How does it work?

Vanilla textures on a player head

Since version 1.21.9, Minecraft Java can override the skin texture of a player head through the minecraft:profile component. A vanilla texture from the Minecraft resource pack can be specified for this.

A player head still uses the geometry and UV mapping of a player's head. Item, block or entity textures may therefore look different from how they normally appear in the game.

Examples

Ready-made commands to copy right away

Farmer (Villager)

/give @s minecraft:player_head[minecraft:profile={texture:"entity/villager/profession/farmer"}]

Stone

/give @s minecraft:player_head[minecraft:profile={texture:"block/stone"}]

Diamond

/give @s minecraft:player_head[minecraft:profile={texture:"item/diamond"}]

Steve

/give @s minecraft:player_head[minecraft:profile={texture:"entity/player/wide/steve"}]

Alex

/give @s minecraft:player_head[minecraft:profile={texture:"entity/player/slim/alex",model:"slim"}]

Drowned outer layer

/give @s minecraft:player_head[minecraft:profile={texture:"entity/zombie/drowned_outer_layer"}]
