Our History

How it all began and where we're going

Who We Are

Founded 2026 Blexilein

MC-Craft was founded in 2026 by me "Blexilein" with the vision of providing high-quality Minecraft tools to the community for free.

Our team consists of Minecraft enthusiasts, developers and designers who all share a common passion: Minecraft and its incredible community.

Our Mission

Vision Goals

At MC-Craft we believe Minecraft is more than just a game - it's a platform for creativity, learning and community.

Provide high-quality tools for free
Support and connect the Minecraft community
Be a trusted resource for all Minecraft topics

What We Offer

Tools Resources

MC-Craft now offers 16 free tools and resources for Minecraft players, including:

Text Converter - Create stylish chat text and formatting codes for your server
Skin Lookup - Find the skin, UUID, and capes for any Minecraft account
Items Database - Comprehensive information on every item and material
Mobs Database - Stats, behavior, and drops for every creature
And many more - from Server Status to the Skin Editor and the Crafting Recipes. Discover all 29 tools
