    Good to know

    • Your file is never uploaded – reading and drawing happen entirely in your browser.
    • The layer slider hides everything above a height. “Show this layer only” helps you rebuild it layer by layer.
    • Hover over a block to see its name, state and position.
    • In the material list, doors, beds and tall plants count as one item, double slabs as two.
    • Jigsaw blocks in Minecraft structures are replaced by their final state, just like in the game.

    How it works

    From file upload to 3D model

    Every common format

    WorldEdit (.schem, .schematic), Litematica (.litematic) and Minecraft structures (.nbt) are detected automatically.

    Real block models

    Stairs, fences, chests, banners and water show up with the models and textures from Minecraft 26.3.

    On to the converter

    Need another format? The Structure Converter turns your file into it right away.
